Boredom Births Creativity

April 29, 2025

Stop Motion Studio, Adobe Premiere Pro, iPhone camera & mic

My final animation project was certainly a challenge. I learned the most as I actually tried to put the project together. World building presented to be the most difficult part of this project for me, as I tried to use a green screen for the first time! As for the content. My theme surrounded the idea that creativity and imagination blooms from boredom, and is fueled by our emotions and environment. I presented the characters using the stuffed animals I already owned. I edited them into my own house instead of building a set. The narrative follows a young girl (or bunny) who is scorned by her mother and sent to her room. Before her phone is taken away, her friend suggests the girl use her imagination to battle the boredom she will feel while she stays in her room the rest of the day. With these two influences, her friend and mother, she finds motivation to have fun and create music. It was a lot of fun trying to make it seem like she was bobbing her head and dancing! True creative minds are diminishing everyday with constant districations and stimulations. Sometimes we just need to be bored in order to force our minds to be and do, rather than just consume.
