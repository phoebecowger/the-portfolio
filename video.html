Blair Witch Challenge

April 19, 2025

Adobe Premiere Pro, iPhone camera

My parody movie trailer is based on The Blair Witch Project. This movie was unique in itself due it being the first of its kind: a found footage horror film. First time viewers even mistook the movie as being real, as the style of the film is conducted as though the characters themselves were recording and documenting the experience themself. Whether or not you liked the movie, it was revolutionary for cinema! I wanted to add a modern twist to the plot, setting the time in 2025 instead of the 1990s. I kept the found-footage style of recording, which included many “random movement” types of shots. And, I kept the 4k quality of my Iphone, rather than the lower-quality film cameras the students from the movie used, as would modern day streamers would do. I altered the characters to be streamers instead of students, and the Blair Witch as a viral social media challenge instead of an ancient legend.
